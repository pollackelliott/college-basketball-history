## Conference history

User-supplied membership timeline:
- Independent through 1909-10
- Southern Intercollegiate Athletic Association (SIAA), 1910-11 through 1920-21
- Southern Conference, 1921-22 through 1931-32
- Southeastern Conference, 1932-33 to present

The detailed Kentucky game history explicitly labels the 1922 event as the SIAA Tournament despite the supplied Southern Conference membership start. The detailed 1924 season labels its postseason event the Southern Conference Tournament, while a separate year-by-year summary footnote appears inconsistent. These source-label nuances are documented but do not change the public classification: the games are conference-tournament games.

## Conference tournaments

- SIAA / Southern Conference tournament games before the SEC: 26
- SEC Tournament games through 2025-26: 170 (138-32 on court)
- Total conference-tournament games in the package: 196
- Championship-game appearances: 44, including both wins and losses

For the SEC, the user supplied the champion/runner-up list and the official Kentucky record book supplies the full game-by-game SEC Tournament history. Every championship game is tagged `Championship`, whether Kentucky won or lost.

Kentucky's 1987-88 SEC regular-season and tournament championships were later stripped as part of NCAA sanctions. The official Kentucky record book explicitly states that Kentucky was not forced to forfeit those regular-season or SEC Tournament games. The project therefore retains the on-court game results and does not mark those SEC games as forfeits/vacated games.
