# Northwestern Source Notes

## Primary historical source

Official Northwestern men's basketball historical/media-guide game log previously extracted during curation.

The historical extraction contains 2,795 played-game rows through 2024-25. Three cancelled rows were excluded. Seven historical played rows have no printed exact date and remain date-unknown rather than guessed.

## 2025-26

The curated 2025-26 seed contains 34 played games and a 15-19 record. Preseason exhibitions against Iowa State and Illinois State were excluded.

## Source hierarchy

1. Northwestern year-by-year game rows
2. Official opponent-school records for cross-source reconciliation
3. Season headings and summary tables as QA evidence
4. Supplemental research only when explicitly needed

## Aggregate-record issue

The guide's 120 season headings sum to 1,191-1,603-1 through 2024-25. Raw printed game-row W/L/T markers sum to 1,183-1,611-1 because of forfeiture/administrative-era treatment. A quick-facts line gives 1,111-1,603-1 and conflicts with the season-by-season section by 80 wins.

Canonical games follow the listed game rows. Administrative effects belong in metadata.

## Opponent identities

All previously flagged opponent ambiguities were resolved, including:
- `Loyola` = Loyola Chicago
- `N’western Coll.` / `N’western College` = Northwestern College, a distinct historical/non-D1 institution and not Northwestern University

NCAA and NIT appearance/game lists were supplied by the user in this conversation. Big Ten Tournament games were identified from Northwestern's existing year-by-year game log. Northwestern has no Big Ten Tournament championship appearance.
