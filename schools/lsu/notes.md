## Conference chronology

LSU's institutional history says the university joined the SIAA in 1896, the Southern Conference in 1922, and became a charter SEC member when the SEC was established. For basketball-package seasons this is represented as SIAA through 1921-22, Southern beginning 1922-23, and SEC beginning 1932-33.

## Venue chronology and source caution

LSU Athletics identifies the Pavilion on the old campus, temporary high-school sites in the late 1920s, the Gym/Armory, the John M. Parker Agricultural Coliseum (Cow Palace), and the Assembly Center/PMAC lineage in its basketball facility history. Independent LSU institutional building history distinguishes the **Gym/Armory** (current Cox Academic Center) from the separate **Huey Long Field House**. LSU basketball publications sometimes conflate those names. This package therefore does **not** use Huey Long Field House as a blanket primary-home identity; it remains a distinct physical venue when explicitly established (including the 1938 SEC Tournament). Late-1920s home rows are left without a blanket physical venue rather than guessed.

Venue chronology is never used to infer H/A/N. It supplies a primary-home venue only after the ledger independently marks a game home.

## Reciprocal-source normalization completed during package QA

Two package-level historical issues were resolved from already-onboarded official reciprocal sources before freezing the portfolio:

- **1916-02-21 at Ole Miss:** LSU's ledger omitted the exact date for the 55-29 win. Ole Miss's official ledger gives Feb. 21, 1916, so the structured LSU date is populated while LSU's raw text remains unchanged.
- **1957-02-18 at Alabama:** LSU's ledger prints `L, 70-62`, an internally impossible score/result combination. Alabama's official ledger gives Alabama 82-70 LSU. The structured LSU score is therefore normalized to 70-82 while preserving LSU's raw text and documenting the reciprocal basis.
