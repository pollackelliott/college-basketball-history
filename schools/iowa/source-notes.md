# Iowa source notes

## Primary institutional source

**Iowa Men's Basketball 2025-26 Media Guide** (owner-supplied PDF: `pgCeN7cmUxRGvYsav8sO4T24PrjObV7rwN3Ys7HQ.pdf`).

The guide's year-by-year score ledger is the backbone for 1901-02 through 2024-25. It is cross-checked against the guide's yearly records, all-time series, NCAA Tournament history, Big Ten championship-team section and postseason history. The guide explicitly defines `*` as home game, `#` as overtime(s), and `+` as awarded forfeit.

Known limitation: the guide is a preseason 2025-26 publication and therefore does not contain the completed 2025-26 season. It also contains a small number of internal transcription inconsistencies documented in `notes.md`; raw source wording is preserved where normalization is required.

## 2025-26 completed-season supplement

Iowa Athletics official 2025-26 schedule/results and official recaps were used to add the completed season. The competitive season was 37 games, 24-13, including two Big Ten Tournament games and four NCAA Tournament games through the Elite Eight. No exhibition was included.

Representative official Iowa Athletics sources used during research:

## Conference tournament site reference

Owner-supplied workbook: **`Conference_Tournament_Site_Reference(5).xlsm`**.

The workbook is globally incomplete. The owner explicitly authorized **only the Big Ten section** as complete for Iowa. The package uses that section for Iowa Big Ten Tournament site/venue assignments from the tournament's 1998 inception through 2026. No unfinished conference section was promoted to canon.

## NCAA Tournament site evidence

The media guide's NCAA Tournament history supplies tournament cities by appearance. Physical venue mapping was cross-checked against the repository's current-main global venue registry and committed NCAA archival evidence layer, whose source authority is NCAA's Division I Men's Basketball Attendance and Sites material. 2026 Tampa/Houston venues were supplemented with Iowa Athletics contemporary recaps.

Where a historical physical venue/ID could not be established to the package's confidence threshold, the package prefers an honest blank rather than inventing a global identity. Such rows remain an integration-time reference prerequisite, not a reason to infer H/A/N.

## Early home facilities

University of Iowa/Iowa Athletics institutional history establishes the Old Armory -> New Armory -> Iowa Field House -> Carver-Hawkeye Arena sequence. Contemporary Daily Iowan material confirms the New Armory was ready for basketball use in 1921 and hosted Iowa basketball in 1921-22. Venue chronology is used only on rows already explicitly designated home by source evidence.

## Opponent identity method

Every distinct source opponent label was normalized through the current program registry where applicable, the media guide's all-time-series context, institutional lineage research for historical aliases, and explicit non-current identities for defunct/local/military/YMCA opponents. Historical labels are not forced into modern institutions without lineage evidence.
