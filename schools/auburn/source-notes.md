# Auburn source notes

## Primary official sources

### Auburn 2025-26 Men's Basketball Media Guide / Record Book

The uploaded **Auburn 25-26 media guide.pdf** is the principal historical source. The package uses its year-by-year results, opponent-series history, SEC Tournament history, NCAA Tournament history, arena records, and program record summaries.

Historical year-by-year results are on printed media-guide pages 98-118. The package preserves each extracted row's printed-page reference and raw source text.

### Auburn Athletics — 1937-38 schedule

https://auburntigers.com/sports/mens-basketball/schedule/season/1937-38

Used only to supplement the media guide's explicitly incomplete 1937-38 ledger with four identifiable score-unknown games. Existing scored media-guide rows remain the primary game evidence.

### Auburn Athletics — 2025-26 schedule

https://auburntigers.com/sports/mens-basketball/schedule/season/2025-26

Used for the completed 2025-26 season. Two exhibition games (Oklahoma State and Memphis) are excluded. The official schedule reports 22-16 overall, 7-11 SEC, 14-4 home, 2-8 away and 6-4 neutral.

### Auburn University Archives — Alumni Gymnasium

https://www.lib.auburn.edu/archive/find-aid/798/00000027.htm

Used with owner approval to date Alumni Gymnasium's dedication to Feb. 22, 1916 and support its primary-home placement beginning in 1916-17.

## Source hierarchy and preservation

1. Auburn media guide chronological/year-by-year ledger.
2. Auburn dedicated series/postseason tables when they clearly resolve an internal media-guide typo or identity.
3. Auburn official season schedule for the 1937-38 incomplete-ledger supplement and completed 2025-26 season.
4. Opponent/other official evidence only for a genuine conflict when needed.

When a source typo is curated, `raw_text` preserves the printed evidence and `notes` explains the correction.

## Known deferred modern conflict

The 2005-03-11 LSU SEC Tournament row differs between Auburn's chronological ledger and SEC Tournament summary. Per owner instruction, this 21st-century discrepancy is carried to consolidated owner review without extended research.
