# Iowa State source notes

## Primary school source
**2025-26 Iowa State Men's Basketball Fact Book** — Iowa State Athletics, supplied PDF.
- Primary authority for 1907-08 through 2024-25 game-by-game results.
- Used for season records, literal opponent labels, dates, scores, H/A/N source signals, conference/postseason markers, home-court chronology, and historical tournament/location footnotes.
- The 118 historical seasons from 1907-08 through 2024-25 were mechanically extracted and every extracted season game count was reconciled to its printed season record.
- The detailed ledger sums to 1,485-1,410 through 2024-25; the Quick Facts aggregate reports 1,485-1,409. The package retains the detailed game ledger and documents the summary discrepancy.

## 2025-26 completed season
**Iowa State Athletics 2025-26 completed schedule / game records**.
- Adds the completed 2025-26 competitive schedule after the fact-book cutoff.
- Two exhibitions are excluded under project policy.
- Adds 37 competitive games and a 29-8 printed-result season; on-court result accounting remains governed by source scores.

## Site research
- Iowa State official home-court history establishes Margaret Hall Gym, State Gym, The Armory, and Hilton Coliseum chronology.
- Iowa State official conference-tournament history and conference/site histories were used for Big Six/Seven/Eight and Big 12 tournament locations.
- NCAA Tournament physical sites were checked against official NCAA archival site evidence and host/venue history as needed.
- NIT and other postseason sites were checked against Iowa State postseason history plus host/arena chronology.
- Neutral regular-season event footnotes in the fact book were actively audited. 120 of 121 regular-season neutral games have exact physical venues. The lone building-level residual is Iowa State-Holy Cross on 1970-12-19 at the Marshall Tournament in Huntington, WV; targeted research established the event/location but not sufficient explicit evidence for the exact building, so the row is marked `RESEARCHED_UNRESOLVED` rather than inferred.
