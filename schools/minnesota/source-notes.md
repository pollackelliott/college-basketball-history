# Minnesota source notes

## 1. Primary source

Primary historical evidence is the University of Minnesota's official
2025-26 Men's Basketball Media Guide, particularly its Year-by-Year Scores
section.

The completed 2025-26 season comes from Minnesota's official athletics
schedule. The two 2025-26 exhibition games are excluded.

## 2. NCAA-sanction era supplement

For 1993-94 through 1998-99 the official Minnesota guide preserves chronology
but suppresses game scores/results. A secondary score/result supplement is
used for those six seasons while Minnesota's official chronology remains the
primary evidence.

The six seasons contain 194 games with a combined result of 127-67. Seven
date disagreements between Minnesota's chronology and the supplement are
preserved explicitly for reconciliation.

## 3. Owner-authoritative project decisions

Owner rulings control Minnesota's site history boundary, conference chronology,
home-venue chronology, accomplishment baseline, and explicit reconciliation
decisions presented by the sealed onboarding review.
