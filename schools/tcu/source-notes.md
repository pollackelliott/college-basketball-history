# TCU men's basketball — source notes

## Primary institutional source

**2025-26 TCU Basketball Fact Book** (owner-supplied `Tcu.pdf`, TCU Athletics).

Used for:
- program history and all-time year-by-year results;
- all-time tournament results;
- NCAA/NIT/CBI postseason history;
- home-facility chronology;
- opponent-series and season-control evidence.

The complete row-level game universe was reconciled against the fact-book season summaries and, where necessary, official TCU season schedules.

## Official TCU web schedules / institutional history

Official TCU Athletics schedules were used for bounded row-level repairs, modern completed-season supplementation, postseason identification, and venue/site verification.

TCU institutional conference history supports:
- TIAA: 1909-22;
- Southwest Conference: 1923-95;
- WAC: 1996-00;
- CUSA: 2001-04;
- Mountain West: 2005-11;
- Big 12: 2012-present.

Official TCU championship history supports the recognized men's basketball conference-title chronology.

## Owner-supplied conference-tournament site worksheet

File: `ct venues.xlsm - Tournament Sites(2).csv`

Authorization is **TCU only**. The worksheet was used only where its conference/season rows actually cover TCU's tournament participation after TCU joined the Southwest Conference. It was not generalized to other schools or conferences and was not used to manufacture missing WAC buildings when the supplied sheet contains no WAC row.

## NCAA / postseason verification

NCAA physical venue + city/state completeness was researched separately and is complete for every TCU NCAA Tournament game.

NIT and other postseason campus/neutral sites were researched proportionately. Surviving non-NCAA physical-building gaps are explicitly research-accounted under project policy rather than inferred.

## Source conflicts and corrections

Important accepted corrections include:
- official TCU schedule evidence repairing omitted/shifted rows from the printed historical tables;
- 1976-77 SWC tournament summary defect corrected from `@Arkansas` to Houston in Fort Worth using stronger TCU season/series evidence;
- 1981 and 1983 SWC first-round games restored to the postseason partition;
- 18 December 1953-60 "Southwest Conference Tournament" games restored to regular-season tournament status under project taxonomy.

Literal source wording remains preserved in `raw_text` even when a curated field uses stronger evidence.

## Research limits

Unknown exact dates and physical venues were not inferred from chronology, geography, or ordinary series patterns.
Away regular-season physical venue gaps are permitted reciprocal debt.
All material HOME, neutral, and postseason gaps are either filled or carry explicit research accounting.


## Stage 5 identity correction

Owner review correctly challenged `Corpus Christi`. Historical University of Corpus Christi is in the
institutional lineage of present-day Texas A&M-Corpus Christi. The package therefore resolves the row to
`texas-a-m-corpus-christi` / `Texas A&M-Corpus Christi` / `current_d1 = Yes`.
