## Bounded unit completed

The startup assumption that 1907-08 was necessarily institutional varsity inception is **not frozen**.

Current official Gonzaga athletics accounting is internally clear: the 2026-27 all-time coaching table begins at 1907-08 (9-2), continues continuously through 2025-26, and reports 119 years. Repeated official athletics history/game-note language also calls 1907-08 the first basketball season/year.

However, bounded target-school and contemporary-history review exposed concrete earlier evidence that cannot be silently discarded:

- Gonzaga University historical archives say basketball activity began in February 1905 after the gym opened, initially without a set schedule/league and with numerous intramural teams.
- A Gonzaga Special Collections historical article says the University began competing in intercollegiate basketball in 1906.
- Spokane historical reporting identifies a 40-5 Gonzaga win over Medical Lake High on December 15, 1905 and references a photograph of the Gonzaga 1905-06 basketball team.
- A contemporaneous January 30, 1907 Spokane report is indexed as Whitman 31, Gonzaga 24.

Because Gonzaga College at the time included preparatory/high-school-age divisions as well as collegiate divisions, the pre-1907 evidence establishes basketball-team activity and at least one intercollegiate contest but does not yet, by itself, prove which season current repository policy should treat as the first recognized **collegiate varsity program** season.
