## One exact-source date correction — Idaho

The [University of Idaho 2025–26 official game-by-game history](https://s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/sidearm.nextgen.sites/govandals.com/documents/2025/5/21/MBB_Record_Book_25-26.pdf) has Idaho W35–24 at Gonzaga on **February 17, 1938**. But the university's **contemporaneous original [February 8, 1938 *Idaho Argonaut*, printed p.3](https://objects.lib.uidaho.edu/argonaut/pdf/arg-1938-02-08.pdf)** (original page screenshot examined) explicitly headlines Idaho reserves defeating Gonzaga 35–24 *last night*, in Spokane. It identifies the university's coaches, reserves, sole first-stringer, and Bulldog high scorer. This establishes Monday **February 7, 1938** for exactly the **same** 35–24 game. The independent Feb8 [Salem Capital Journal contemporaneous AP report](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn90066132/1938-02-08/ed-1/seq-3/) also dates the game to the previous night; its OCR says 35–34, but the Idaho original scanned source definitively prints **35–24**, so no extra game or alternative score is inferred.

Successor research ledger `stage1-identified-game-ledger-0038.csv` changes **one already accepted** row `GONZAGA-EXP-IDAHO-009`: date `1938-02-17` → **`1938-02-07`** with source/provenance documentation.
