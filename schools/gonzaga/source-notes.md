## Stage 6 NCAA round self-challenge

The Stage 6 adversarial audit found all 76 NCAA Tournament rows had blank `curated_postseason_round` values. This was repaired as one bounded school-wide round-classification defect without reopening site research. For every non-2020-21 appearance, the accepted Gonzaga institutional NCAA source legend preserved in the Stage 3B parent identifies the tournament phase family (First/Second Round, Regional, or Final Four); chronological progression within that supported tournament run was standardized to the repository labels `R64`, `R32`, `Sweet Sixteen`, `Elite Eight`, `Final Four`, and `Championship`. Gonzaga had no First Four appearance in the included NCAA runs. The 2020-21 all-Indianapolis tournament, whose Gonzaga legend is generic, was separately checked against the NCAA official 2021 bracket (https://www.ncaa.com/_flysystem/public-s3/images/2023/02/24/2021NCAATBracket.pdf), which explicitly labels first round, second round, Sweet 16, Elite 8, national semifinals, and championship and places Gonzaga on that path. The 2025-26 opening progression was also checked against the NCAA official 2026 printable bracket (https://www.ncaa.com/brackets/print/basketball-men/d1/2026), confirming Gonzaga's Kennesaw State game in the first round and Texas game in the second round. No round was assigned from geography, opponent identity, or a modern naming assumption alone.
