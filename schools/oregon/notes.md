## Source defects and conflicts preserved

The package preserves raw Oregon source strings while normalizing structured fields only when evidence supports the correction. Examples include:
- malformed date typography (`F I 2` -> 1932-02-12; `E 14` -> 1953-02-14; `4 01` -> 2011-04-01; lowercase `f 6` -> 2021-02-06);
- the 1926 San Jose State reciprocal score disagreement described above;
- source-internal H/A/N summary discrepancies. Game-level star/event notation and, for modern seasons, Oregon Athletics' completed schedule take precedence over mechanically forcing a contradictory season summary. For example, Oregon Athletics' completed 2021-22 schedule reports 12-5 at home, consistent with 17 game-level home rows, while the record-book summary line says 12-4.
- later forfeiture notes are preserved administratively without overwriting on-court truth: 1975-12-30 Oregon State and 1996-01-20 California.
