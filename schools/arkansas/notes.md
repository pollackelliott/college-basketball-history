## Arkansas statewide home-game policy

Standalone Arkansas-scheduled games in Arkansas cities away from Fayetteville are classified as Arkansas Home, including Little Rock, North Little Rock, Pine Bluff, Fort Smith, Blytheville, and El Dorado.

Clearly identified tournament/event games would remain neutral. Exact historical alternate-city venues are not guessed when the guide supplies only a city/location abbreviation.
