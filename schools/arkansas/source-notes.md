# Arkansas Source Notes

## Primary historical source

Official Arkansas Men's Basketball Media Guide 2025-2026.

The year-by-year game logs are the primary historical source for dates, opponents, scores, results, and source site notation.

Summary tables and opponent-series summaries are QA evidence only. When they conflict with a season's game-by-game log and the game log reconciles, the game log controls unless stronger official evidence resolves a specific typo.

## Historical extraction

The historical extraction contains 2,849 non-exhibition game rows across 102 completed seasons through 2024-25. All 102 season game counts and W-L totals reconcile to the year-by-year history.

Known source-summary inconsistencies were retained as internal QA rather than used to invent or delete games.

## 2025-26

The curated 2025-26 seed contains 37 non-exhibition games and a 28-9 record. Two preseason exhibitions were excluded.

## Conference membership

Arkansas was in the Southwest Conference through 1990-91 and joined the SEC beginning in 1991-92.

## Postseason sources

The Arkansas guide includes consolidated SEC Tournament history, NCAA Tournament history, year-by-year SWC Tournament blocks, and NIT results.

Official guide aggregate checks through 2024-25:
- Conference tournament: 55-42
- SWC Tournament: 23-10
- SEC Tournament: 32-32
- NCAA Tournament: 52-36
- NIT: 6-5

The 2025-26 schedule adds a 3-0 SEC Tournament championship run and a 2-1 NCAA Sweet Sixteen run.

## Venue sources

Arkansas guide/history establishes the primary home chronology:
- Schmidt Gymnasium through 1936-37
- Men's Gymnasium / Field House beginning 1937-38
- Barnhill Arena beginning 1954-55 through 1992-93
- Bud Walton Arena first official game Nov. 29, 1993

For statewide Arkansas home games, the guide often provides only a city/location abbreviation. Exact venue names are intentionally left blank unless supported.

## Cross-source evidence

Official Missouri, Illinois, Kansas, and Kentucky histories were used as independent evidence for shared games. Northwestern has no games against Arkansas in the curated history.

Contemporary official Arkansas/Kentucky/Missouri schedule or box-score pages were used where later record-book rows conflict on modern scores or sites.

## Source preservation

A source assertion is not silently rewritten merely because another source establishes the canonical value. For example, Arkansas's 2025-26 guide assertion of 51-63 vs Kentucky on Jan. 27, 2024 remains preserved as evidence even though the canonical final is 57-63.
