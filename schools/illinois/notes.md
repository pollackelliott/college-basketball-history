# Illinois Curation Notes

## Program status

Illinois is curated through the 2025-26 season. Material unresolved cross-source conflicts are preserved rather than guessed.

## Canonical competitive coverage

- 3,040 non-exhibition games through 2025-26
- On-court record: 1,955-1,085
- Historical through 2024-25: 3,003 games, 1,929-1,074
- 2025-26: 37 games, 28-9
- Exhibitions are excluded from public totals.

## Conference history

Illinois is normalized as a Big Ten member throughout its varsity basketball history. Conference tournament games use `Big Ten Tournament`.

## Postseason tagging

- Big Ten Tournament: 59 games
- NCAA Tournament: 87 games
- NIT: 17 games

Only Big Ten Tournament or NIT championship games receive `postseason_round = Championship`. NCAA rounds use the project bracket-stage taxonomy.

The March 14, 1951 Kansas State `NCAA Warmup Game` is excluded from Illinois' official NCAA Tournament aggregate, so it remains a regular-season/special-event source row.

Historical NCAA consolation and third-place games remain `NCAA_TOURNAMENT` with a blank canonical round.

## Cross-source reconciliation

Illinois has been cross-checked against Missouri, Northwestern, Kansas, Kentucky, and Arkansas where histories overlap.

Standing items include the unresolved 1932-33 Missouri-Illinois exact-date conflict and a small number of older Illinois-Northwestern date/score/site conflicts. February 3, 2001 is the user-resolved Illinois-Northwestern date. Braggin' Rights games in St. Louis are neutral.

Two 1976-77 Minnesota games were later forfeited to Illinois. The on-court scores/results are retained, with the administrative note preserved separately in source metadata.
