## Stage 6 bounded self-challenge evidence
### Exact-date repair
- `UCF-R-200607-029` vs Houston:
  - literal primary year-by-year evidence remains `F29 Houston W 75-72 (ot)`;
  - 2007 has no February 29;
  - UCF official contemporaneous recap identifies the game as Feb. 28, 2007 and confirms the 75-72 overtime result:
    https://ucfknights.com/news/2007/02/28/golden-knights-top-houston-clinch-second-in-c-usa
  - UCF's later official season retrospective independently references the Houston win on Feb. 28:
    https://ucfknights.com/news/2007/05/28/a-look-back-at-ucfs-top-moments-from-2006-07
The curated `game_date` is therefore 2007-02-28 while literal source evidence remains preserved.

### HOME venue challenge
- UCF official retrospective places the 1986 Campbell Merrill Lynch Classic game at Valencia Community College:
  https://ucfknights.com/news/2006/12/17/ucf-to-compete-at-san-juan-shootout
- Campbell institutional media-guide venue abbreviations identify `VCC` as Valencia Community College (Orlando, FL).
- Valencia College institutional history confirms West Campus opened in 1971 and East Campus in 1975:
  https://valenciacollege.edu/about/history.php
- Archival Orange County material identifies a Valencia Community College West Campus gymnasium, but no authoritative game-level evidence establishes that the 1986 UCF event used that specific building.
The 1986 building therefore remains unresolved. The 1987 Merrill Lynch Classic is similarly supported to Orlando/HOME but not to a specific building; adjacent event editions cannot safely be propagated.
