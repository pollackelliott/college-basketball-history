## Systematic institutional season-accounting discovery

Official Mountain West Conference men's basketball record books (2024-25 edition and 2025-26 edition) carry a season-by-season Boise State table. All **33** listed junior-college seasons, 1933-34 through 1967-68 except 1937-38 and 1938-39, sum exactly to **441 wins and 346 losses**, **787 games**. This independently reproduces the official Boise State Athletics junior-college aggregate 441-346 (1933-1968). 

- MW 2024-25: https://storage.googleapis.com/themw-com/2024/08/3b5fdeec-2024-25-mbb-record-book.pdf
- MW 2025-26: https://storage.googleapis.com/themw-com/2026/01/a6922d42-2025-26-mw-mbb-record-book.pdf
- Boise State Athletics: https://broncosports.com/news/2007/4/5/men_s_basketball_tradition

This is annual aggregate authority, **not** evidence for 787 invented anonymous games. The annual table's earliest recognized season is **1933-34, 6-2 (Staton Hale)**. University Archives also dates basketball activity to December 1932; therefore 1932-33 remains an inception/season-boundary research question and is not silently reclassified PRE_INCEPTION. The MW yearly list omits 1937-38 and 1938-39; the exact aggregate sum is strong accounting evidence but omission alone is not authoritative no-team proof. These three unlisted seasons remain explicit separate uncertainty. 

The annual records for 1954-55 (18-11) and 1956-57 (25-6) differ from retrospective NJCAA placement-record summaries sometimes reported as 21-7 (1955) and 23-5 (1957). Record definitions and mid-tournament snapshots must be reconciled, not overwritten.
