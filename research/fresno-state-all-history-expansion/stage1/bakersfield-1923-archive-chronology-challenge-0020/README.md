## Targeted institutional and archival historical challenge
1. A much earlier Bakersfield institution, Bakersfield Junior College, is established in early twentieth-century educational records and Bakersfield College's official historical accounts; modern CSU Bakersfield was not operating in 1922–23. The 1922–23 school could not be the present CSU Bakersfield Roadrunners. Fresno State's own historical *football* record book says it played `Bakersfield JC` in 1922, 1923, and 1924 (prior checkpoint 0015). **Cross-sport association is plausible context, not a 70–13 basketball score link.**
2. Bakersfield College's official archives preserve student newspaper *Renegade Rip / Rene-K-Gade* digitizations with a purported early item indexed **January 8, 1923**, and later material. In this bounded unit, the archive's item was **actually opened and visually inspected** at pages **0, 1, 2, and 3**. The cover says `Tuesday, January 8`, and later internal material has a `Tuesday, January 15` issue header. Gregorian calendar cross-check: **January 8, 1923 was Monday**, whereas **January 8 and 15, 1924 were Tuesdays**. The catalog's `1923-January-8.pdf` filename/date is therefore contradicted by the internal weekday+month/day metadata; the scanned pages plausibly belong to **January 1924**. Do not assert an exact year solely from the weekday, nor use this 1923-labeled archival container as a confirmed 1922–23 match report.
3. The newspaper scan contains early-1920s school and sports material, including contemporary Fresno State football/conference references and *high school* basketball practice commentary. **No Fresno vs Bakersfield basketball result of 70–13, named 1922–23 basketball opponent, or game date was authenticated in the visually inspected pages.** The scanned archive issue is not exhaustive for all 1922–23 publications or for the rest of its own pages.
4. Bakersfield College's *Raconteur* yearbooks are digitally archived only from **1932 forward**; the 1923 *Scorpion* is referenced historically on the official Grace Van Dyke Bird archive page, but no 1923 basketball section or actual 1923 scorebook was retrieved. Do **not** use a 1932+ sports yearbook to backfill a 1923 exact result.
5. Fresno State's newly indexed university history describes early California Coast Conference participation with several junior colleges beginning 1922. A conference-history secondary description also places Bakersfield JC and Fresno State together in 1923. These facts support era proximity and credible athletic contact only; neither is exact game proof. The two separate universities' modern series pages cannot prove the 1923 game's identity, and the mcubed copy of the Fresno guide is not independent corroboration.
6. Bakersfield Junior College historically shared a campus with Bakersfield High School/Kern County Union High School. This means institutional and local high-school sports identities existed in parallel. Do not silently equate a generic `Bakersfield` historical referent with either school. The Fresno guide's retrospective `CSU Bakersfield` is *anachronistic*; the precise replacement remains `UNKNOWN_HISTORICAL_BAKERSFIELD_OR_OTHER` until a directly matching dated source is read.
