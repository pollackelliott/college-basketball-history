### Scope and caution

AIRPAC was not established until 1942, so a 1949 `Air Pac (Fleet Air)` team is not automatically the exact same historical military identity as a generic 1922 `Fleet Air` opponent. Navy/Miramar command chronology distinguishes 1943 Kearney Flyers from 1946 Miramar NAS Kearny Fliers, and from Miramar Marines. MCRD differs from the previously accepted broad San Diego Marines source series. Distinct local Coast Guard basketball teams are not collapsed; no submarine number is invented.

New Mexico School of Mines is the ancestor of New Mexico Tech, while New Mexico Western College is now Western New Mexico University. Southwestern University (Los Angeles) was a 1920s multi-department institution with sports teams and is now Southwestern Law School. Alderson-Broaddus in 1956 is a documented NAIA postseason opponent. No original result, event category, H/A/N, venue or accepted competitive game population changed.
